Scoring & rules

Points: full completions use the exact Pointercrate point schedule for ranks #1–150 (for example, #1 = 350.00, #2 = 331.71, #75 = 40.60 and #150 = 18.71). Progress awards 10% of the level’s completion value. Totals are calculated to two decimal places and recalculate when ranks change.

Progress: every ranked level accepts qualifying progress records from 50% to 99%. A qualifying progress record earns one tenth of that level’s completion points. Only each player’s highest approved percentage on a level counts.

No double counting: progress and completion points never stack for the same level. When a player completes a level, their progress points for it are removed and replaced by the full completion points.

Records: full completions and percentage runs need a proof link and are reviewed before counting.

V6 achievements: badges are based on approved full completions, list ranks and points; progress percentages do not unlock achievements. The database migration file beta-v5-database-update.sql adds fields needed to save verifier details and the rank at the time of a completion; run it in Supabase before testing those two achievements with live data.

Privacy: the public site shows display names and records, not sign-in email addresses. Shared editing requires an approved member account and the database setup.
