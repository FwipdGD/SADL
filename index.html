Scoring & rules

Points: a unique completion earns 1000 ÷ rank points (rounded to one decimal). #1 gives 1000, #5 gives 200, and #10 gives 100. Points recalculate when list ranks change.

Records: one completion per player per level. Add proof links so the group can check records.

Privacy: the public site shows display names and records, not sign-in email addresses. Shared editing requires an approved member account and the database setup.

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Pointercrate or RobTop Games.
