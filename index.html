List standards

Agree the ranking as a group. Require credible completion proof, be clear about platform/mod rules, and resolve disputes before changing a rank. Only add real completions to the public list.

04 / Rules & integrity

Unofficial community list. SADL is a friends-only ranking project, not an official Geometry Dash leaderboard.

Suggested rule: a level is ranked only after at least one member has beaten it and shared proof. Rank by the group's agreed difficulty order, not just by the game's difficulty label. Keep progress records separate from completed levels.

Editing: in local preview, edits affect this browser only. Once connected to a shared database, use member sign-ins and database permissions rather than putting a shared password or secret key in the website.
