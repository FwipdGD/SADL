Scoring & rules

Points: a full completion earns 350 ÷ rank points (rounded to one decimal). #1 gives 350, #5 gives 70, and #10 gives 35. Points recalculate when list ranks change.

Progress: percentage records from 50% to 99% earn one tenth of the level’s full points (so 35 points on #1); a 100% completion earns the full points. Only approved records count.

Records: full completions and percentage runs need a proof link and are reviewed before counting.

Privacy: the public site shows display names and records, not sign-in email addresses. Shared editing requires an approved member account and the database setup.
